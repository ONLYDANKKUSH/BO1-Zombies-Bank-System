# BO1-Zombies-Bank-System
It now saves your points after closing the plutonium app and saves into your t5 folder under the "C:\Users"YOURUSERNAME"\AppData\Local\Plutonium\storage\t5\players and in the text document "config.cfg". Inside the text document if you hit ctrl f and search "snd_khz" you will see your saved points and you can adjust it to your liking.
